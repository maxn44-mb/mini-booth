Support

Need a hand? Tap Send feedback in Mini Booth's Settings, or email maxatminibooth@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model if you can.

How do tokens work?

You get five tokens. Each new print takes one, whether it's a strip or an instant photo. A token comes back 24 hours after you use it. Mini Booth Pro has no limit.

What does Mini Booth Pro include?

  • Unlimited prints
  • Prints made from your own photos
  • The 2×2 grid and double strip
  • More paper colours, and no watermark
  • Every Funhouse effect
  • Smart Start: begin with a raised hand, or when everyone's in the frame
  • The Home Screen widget
  • Every future Pro feature

It's a single purchase, with no subscription.

How do I get Mini Booth Pro back on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in Mini Booth and tap Restore Purchase, under Mini Booth Pro. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

The camera isn't working

Open your iPhone's Settings, tap Apps, then Mini Booth, and turn on Camera.

My instant photo won't develop

Wave your iPhone to develop it. To have photos develop on their own, turn off Wave to develop in Mini Booth's Settings.

Where are my prints?

In the Library, inside Mini Booth. Save adds a print to Photos. Deleting Mini Booth also deletes any prints you haven't saved.

Which iPhones can run it?

Any iPhone with iOS 27 or later. It's at its best on iPhone Duo.
