Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Mini Booth doesn't collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and no server for anything to be sent to.

Your photos and prints

The photos you take and the prints you make are stored in Mini Booth's own storage on your iPhone. They stay there unless you choose to save or share them. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, they're part of that backup, like any app's data. They're removed when you delete them in the Library or delete the app.

The camera

Mini Booth uses the camera to show you what it sees and to take your photos. What the camera sees is processed on your iPhone and isn't sent anywhere.

Faces and hands

To place Funhouse effects and frame your prints, Mini Booth finds faces in your photos and in the camera's picture, using tools built into iOS. If you turn on Smart Start, it also looks for a raised hand or for faces holding steady. All of this happens on your iPhone. Nothing about anyone's face or hands is kept, and none of it is sent anywhere.

Your photo library

Mini Booth can add prints to your photo library when you ask it to, and it can't look through your library. If you make a print from your own photos, you choose them in the system's photo picker, and Mini Booth only receives the ones you choose.

Buying Mini Booth Pro

Mini Booth Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment, and Mini Booth never sees your payment details: it only learns from the App Store whether Pro has been bought. Apple's own privacy policy covers that purchase.

Free tokens

To count free prints, Mini Booth keeps a small record on your iPhone: a random number and a time for each token in use. A copy is kept in your iPhone's Keychain, so that deleting and reinstalling the app doesn't start the count again, and it can stay there after the app is deleted. It says nothing about you or your photos, and it never leaves your iPhone.

The Home Screen widget

If you add the widget, small copies of your latest prints are kept on your iPhone for it to show. They aren't sent anywhere.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You control this in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements. Mini Booth adds nothing to them. If you're testing Mini Booth with TestFlight, Apple also shares crash reports and any feedback you send.

Sending feedback

If you tap Send feedback in Mini Booth's Settings, an email opens in your own mail app with the app version and iOS version filled in. You can change any of it, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. If you do, your email address and your message are used only to reply to you and to improve Mini Booth.

Children

Mini Booth doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
